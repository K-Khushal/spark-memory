# Spark only saves and searches; generative AI lives in Connected assistants

Spark has no chat, summaries or other generative features. Its job is to Capture Memories and make them findable. Users who want to chat about or summarise their Library connect their own AI tool through Spark's MCP server, which exposes search and retrieval only. This keeps Spark from turning into an AI summariser, keeps AI costs per user predictable, and leaves open-ended AI spend with the user's own provider.

## Consequences

- AI output (image descriptions, OCR text, Tags) is used for search and for "why this matched" hints. It never replaces the original Memory.
- Spark pays for Indexing every Memory. That is the only AI cost Spark carries, and it must stay a small, one-time cost per Memory.
