Pehle toor dal ko dho ke pressure cooker mein 3 seeti tak pakao, haldi aur namak ke saath. Alag kadhai mein ghee garam karo, usme jeera, hing aur sukhi lal mirch daalo. Lehsun ko halka brown hone do. Ye tadka dal ke upar daalo aur upar se hara dhaniya aur thoda nimbu nichod do. Chawal ya jeera rice ke saath garam garam khao. Nani kehti thi ki tadka aakhir mein hi daalna chahiye, warna khushboo chali jaati hai.
